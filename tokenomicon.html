Tokenomicon

Every token you summoned. And what it cost you.

Summon your sessions

Tokenomicon scans the transcripts Claude Code keeps on your machine. Pick one session or a pile of them and it breaks down exactly what they cost: input, output, cache reads, cache writes, the works. Nothing leaves your computer.

Drop your ~/.claude/projects folder (or some .jsonl files, or a cloud export) here
  1. Fastest: run python3 tokenomicon.py --serve. It scans your logs and opens this page with everything loaded.
  2. Or hit Load folder and pick ~/.claude/projects. On a Mac, press Cmd+Shift+. in the picker to see hidden folders.
  3. Sessions from the desktop app, the web, your phone or project threads run in Claude's cloud and never land on your disk. Drop a tokenomicon-cloud.json export here (or next to tokenomicon.py) to see those too.
  4. Or poke around with Demo data first. Zero judgment.

Summon your sessions

Tokenomicon reads the transcripts Claude Code already keeps on your machine and tells you exactly how many tokens you burned, what it cost, and how long you spent arguing with it. Nothing leaves your browser.

Drop your ~/.claude/projects folder (or some .jsonl files) here
  1. Fastest: run python3 tokenomicon.py. It scans your logs and opens a report with everything baked in.
  2. Or hit Load folder and pick ~/.claude/projects. On a Mac, press Cmd+Shift+. in the picker to see hidden folders.
  3. Or just poke around with Demo data first. Zero judgment.
